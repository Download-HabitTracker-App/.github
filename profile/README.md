## 🚀 Summary
Habit Tracker for Windows is a focused desktop app for building routines and keeping them going. It brings daily, weekly, and monthly tracking periods into one place, so you can run a daily habit tracker for the everyday basics and a monthly habit tracker for long-term goals without switching tools. Streaks make progress feel rewarding, reminders keep you on time, the habit journal holds your reflections, and progress charts show your consistency at a glance. All of it stays local on your Windows PC, so your habit data remains private and always available.

## ✨ Feature Set
1. **Daily, weekly, and monthly habits** — Cover every tracking period so a daily habit tracker and a weekly habit tracker sit together in one workspace 📅
2. **Streaks** — Keep your streak alive and stay motivated as each completion extends your run 🔥
3. **Reminders** — Set reminders that prompt you at the right time so habits get done consistently ⏰
4. **Habit journal** — Capture notes and reflections in a habit tracker journal beside every check-in 📝
5. **Progress charts** — Turn your history into progress charts that reveal trends and celebrate consistency 📊
